# Funding the devnet environment

The `dev` environment is where the team tests live. Every chain in it is a
test chain except Arc, which has no public testnet any more: Arc is its own
mainnet with a hard $25 per-buy cap in the router, the API and the UI.

The connect sheet in the app shows the same facts per chain (environment,
chain id, gas unit, faucet link) before any wallet prompt.

## How much a tester needs

- One launch and ten trades on a testnet: about 0.05 ETH (Base, RH) or 0.5 SOL.
- Arc: 5–10 USDC covers gas for dozens of transactions; keep buys at 1–10 USDC.

## Team wallets

Use throwaway keys for testnets. The key that deployed RH 46630 and Base
Sepolia was pasted into chat once and is **burned for mainnet**
(`programs/evm/deployments/46630.json`); the Arc deployer must be a fresh
key kept out of chat, tickets and screenshots.

## What "deployed" means per chain

`apps/web/public/chains.json` (generated by `node scripts/emit-chains.mjs`
from `programs/*/deployments/*.json`) is the record of what exists in each
environment. A net missing from it shows "NOT DEPLOYED ON THIS ENV YET" in
the network picker and the API refuses to prepare trades there.
